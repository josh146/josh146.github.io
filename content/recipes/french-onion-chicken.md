All the flavours of French onion soup... with a roast chicken on top. The spatchcocked chicken
roasts directly on a bed of caramelized onions, white wine, and beef stock, and finished adding gruyere under the grill until bubbling. Serve with buttery mashed potatoes and a green salad.

Adapted from Ed Cudlipp's [French onion chicken](https://www.robertwelch.com/blogs/news/french-onion-chicken) for Robert Welch.

## Ingredients

### Chicken

* 1 whole chicken (approximately 1.6-1.8kg)
* 30g unsalted butter (softened)
* 3 cloves garlic (minced)
* 1 tsp fresh thyme leaves
* 2 tbsp olive oil
* Salt

### French onion sauce

* 5 large brown onions (about 1-1.25kg, thinly sliced)
* 40g unsalted butter
* 1 tbsp olive oil
* 1 tsp salt
* 1 tsp sugar
* 4 cloves garlic (thinly sliced)
* 4 sprigs fresh thyme
* 1 tbsp plain flour
* 150ml dry white wine (such as Sauvignon Blanc)
* 300ml [[beef stock]]
* 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
* 1 tsp dijon mustard
* Salt (to taste)
* 200g gruyere cheese (coarsely grated)

## Steps

### Prepare the chicken

1. Spatchcock the chicken. Cut along both sides of the backbone (save the backbone for [[chicken stock]]),
flip the chicken over, and push down on the breastbone to flatten the chicken. Use a paper towel to dry the skin thoroughly.

1. Mix the butter and ingredients to form a herb butter. Separate the chicken skin from the breasts,
and place the butter under the skin. Sprinkle approximately 1 tbsp of salt over the skin of the
entire chicken.

1.  Place in the fridge for a minimum of 1 hour, but preferably overnight.

### Caramelized onions

1. In a large ovenproof frying pan, heat the butter and olive oil over medium heat. Add the onions
and cook, stirring often, until they soften (about 8-10 minutes). Reduce to medium-low, add the
salt and sugar, and cook, stirring frequently, until deeply golden, soft, and jammy (about 50
minutes). If the onions begin to catch, add a small splash of water and scrape up the caramelized
bits.

### French onion sauce

1. Add the sliced garlic and thyme sprigs to the onions and cook for 1-2 minutes until fragrant.
Sprinkle over the flour and stir for another minute to coat the onions.

1. Pour in the white wine, scraping any caramelized bits from the bottom of the pan, and simmer until
reduced by about half.

1. Add the beef stock, Worcestershire sauce, and dijon mustard, and simmer gently for about 5
minutes, until the sauce has thickened slightly. Season with salt and pepper,
and remove from the heat.

### Roast the chicken

1. Preheat the oven to 200ºC.

1. Place the chicken skin-side up directly on top of the French onion sauce. Roast until the skin
is deeply golden and crisp (about 50-55
minutes)[ref]Check the onions occasionally while roasting --- if the sauce begins to dry out, add a
splash of stock or water around the edges of the pan.[/ref].

1. Remove the chicken from the oven and switch the oven to the griller. Scatter the gruyere over the
onions around the chicken, keeping the chicken skin uncovered. Grill for 2-4 minutes, watching
carefully, until the cheese is melted, bubbling, and golden in places.

1. Rest for 10 minutes, then carve and serve, spooning the cheesy onions and sauce over the
chicken.
