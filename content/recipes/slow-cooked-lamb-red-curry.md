This is a delicious Sri Lankan curry made with lamb shanks, adapted from [*Rambutan* by Cynthia Shanmugalingam](/cookbooks?recipe=Slow-cooked+lamb+red+curry&book=Rambutan%3A+Recipes+from+Sri+Lanka&tags=show). The main modifications include the use of tomato paste, and a tadka
instead of Sri Lankan meat powder (a blend of cardamom, fennel, cloves, cinnamon,
and nutmeg). I also swapped out the coconut milk for cream.

## Ingredients

* 4 bone-in lamb shanks (about 1kg)[ref]You can substitude this for a 1.5kg boneless lamb shoulder (cut into 4cm pieces). For a whole bone-in lamb shoulder, increase the cooking time to 4-5 hours.[/ref]
* 300g potatoes (peeled, chopped into eighths)
* 1 red onion (finely diced)
* 6 garlic cloves (minced)
* 6cm ginger (minced)
* 1 tbsp mustard seeds
* 30 fresh curry leaves
* 1 tbsp tomato paste
* 2 tsp salt
* 2 tbsp Sri Lankan roasted curry powder
* Water
* 300ml coconut cream
* 1 lime (or tamarind juice)

### Tadka

* 2 tbsp oil
* 2 tbsp Kashmiri chilli powder
* 10 curry leaves

### Sri Lankan curry powder

The following makes a large quantity of curry powder; this will store well in the fridge in a
ziplock bag. Feel free to scale the following ingredients down.

* 30g coriander seeds
* 15g cumin seeds
* 15g fennel seeds
* 15g black peppercorns
* 2 tbsp vegetable oil
* 8-10 curry leaves
* 70g dried Kashmiri or medium red hot chillies
* 1/2 tsp ground tumeric

## Steps

### Curry powder

1. Toast the coriander, cumin, fennel, and black peppercorns in a frying pan for 1-2 minutes until
fragrant. Remove and set aside.

1. Add oil to the pan, and cook the curry leaves and dried chillies for 2-3 minutes. Remove from the heat, and allow to cool.

1. Using a food processor or spice grinder, blend the coriander, cumin, fennel, pepper, chillies, and curry leaves until a fine powder.

1. Stir in the ground tumeric.

### Curry

1. Heat vegetable oil in a large saucepan (or a pressure cooker on saute mode) over medium heat. Add
the onions and mustard seeds, and cook for 5 minutes or so until just starting to brown. Add the
curry leaves and tomato paste, and cook for a further minute. Add the garlic and ginger, and cook
for 1-2 minutes until fragrant.

1. Add the salt and curry powder and mix. Add the lamb shanks, and add water until roughly half the
lamb shanks are covered. Bring to a simmer and slow cook for 2-3 hours, or pressure cook on high
for 45 minutes. When done, the lamb should be tender and can be cut with a spoon, and the bones
easily removable --- however the meat should hold its form and not disintegrate.

1. While the lamb is cooking, parboil the potatoes (about 10 minutes at a rolling boil). Drain, and
leave aside.

1. When the lamb is done, remove the lamb shanks. Remove the bones and roughly cut the meat.

1. Place the curry sauce over medium-high heat, and add the potatoes and the coconut cream. and
Allow the sauce to boil and thicken over medium heat for about 15-20 minutes. Add the lime
juice (or tamarind), taste and season with salt, and pour the thickened sauce over the lamb.

1. In a small saucepan, heat up the oil and add the curry leaves. Remove from the heat, and stir through the kashmiri chilli powder. Pour over the curry. Serve with rice or [[roti prata]].
